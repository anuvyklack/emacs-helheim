Modules in this directory provide one of the best outliner and personal
knowledge management systems fully preconfigured.

- *Offline-first* — your data belongs to you.
- *Unmatched editing capabilities* — no note app offers fully capable
  Helix/Kakoune-style editing of your notes.
- *Rich markup* — Org-mode is more powerful than Markdown, the best
  code-snippet support around, task management, and tables with multiline
  cells and Excel-like formulas.
- *File attachments* on outline nodes — usable as a personal filesystem.
- *Bidirectional links* for seamless navigation.
- *Daily notes* — your main scratchpad and a better "Inbox".

For me, Org-mode with bidirectional links (provided by [[https://www.orgroam.com/][Org-roam]] or [[https://github.com/meedstrom/org-node][Org-node]]),
daily notes, and the ability to [[https://orgmode.org/manual/Attachments.html][attach files]] to outline nodes split life into
"before" and "after": the first knowledge system I've kept for more than three
years instead of abandoning in weeks.
